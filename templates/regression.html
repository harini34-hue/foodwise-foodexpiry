What is Linear Regression?

Linear Regression is a mathematical and statistical technique used to model the relationship between an independent variable and a dependent variable.

In our project, storage temperature is used as the independent variable and expected shelf life is used as the dependent variable.

Variables Used

X Storage Temperature (°C)
Y Expected Shelf Life (Days)
ŷ Predicted Shelf Life
